Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Idle Call Center does not collect, sell, or use personal data for advertising or tracking.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Local game data

Game progress, settings, and purchase-entitlement information are stored locally on the device.

Game Center

Idle Call Center uses Apple Game Center to provide a global leaderboard. Game Center services and account information are provided and managed by Apple. The developer does not use Game Center information for advertising or tracking.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store payment-card or billing information.

Contact

If you contact the developer by email, the information you voluntarily provide will be used only to respond to your message.

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to staroldur@gmail.com.
